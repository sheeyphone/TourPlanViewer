## Driving notes

- 5-day loop (~1116 km, 10/03–10/07): Tromsø → Lødingen → Lofoten (north & south) → Sørvågen → Harstad → Tromsø
- Lofoten is a dead-end peninsula (same road in/out); one day to cross it, pick the highlights
- ~10–11h of daylight in October — start early, stop before dark
- Backup: Lødingen ↔ Bognes free ferry; Narvik gondola closed in October; Å bakery open until 10/12
